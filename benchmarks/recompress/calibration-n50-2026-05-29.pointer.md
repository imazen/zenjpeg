## Provenance

- **Corpus:** 50 CID22-512 references (first 50 sorted from the training
  split), 512×512, 4:2:0.
- **Source encoders (pinned):** libjpeg-turbo 3.1.0, mozjpeg 4.1.5,
  cjpegli — q20–95 step 5 (16 source-q per image = 800 sources/encoder).
- **Recompressor:** `zjr-calibrate` at the Lever-2 commit
  (Robidoux-aware Preserve for mozjpeg/ImageMagick + picker
  prefer-margin), `HybridMaxCompression` re-encode preset.
- **Metric:** `zen-metrics zensim-gpu` (cuda), Profile A.
- **Regenerated by:** `scripts/recalibrate.sh /tmp/zjr-orig50 <work> 5`.

## Resolved issue

The first n=50 `jpegli_cumulative_420.tsv` (smart-router) collapsed
`fit_calibration.py` to a degenerate 1×1 grid (one BA bucket `[6.7]`,
one target `[50.0]`): the router routed most cells away from Tuned, so
the per-cell Tuned medians were starved. **Fixed** with `cumulative-sweep
--force-tuned` (pins Tuned at every cell). `src/calibration/data.rs`'s
4:2:0 grid was regenerated as a fully-populated 8×8 table (no NaN, adds
target=90). The 4:4:4 grid is still the prior 15-image fit pending a
`--subsampling 444 --force-tuned` run. jpegli uses `data.rs` as its
primary Tuned path (per-encoder `invert_dial` returns `None` for it).
